BEYOND CLICKS & TRAFFIC

Find the gaps in your acquisition process.

Running advertising campaigns doesn't automatically mean your business is acquiring the right customers. Poor targeting, weak offers, disconnected tracking, and ineffective follow-up can increase costs without improving sales opportunities.

Your campaigns generate clicks but few qualified leads.

Your targeting, keywords, ad messaging, or landing pages may not match what your ideal customers are looking for.

Your cost per lead keeps increasing.

Unfocused targeting, wasted search terms, weak creative performance, and poor conversion paths can reduce advertising efficiency.

Your conversion tracking doesn't reflect actual results.

Ad platforms may report leads or conversions that don't match your CRM, qualified opportunities, or sales outcomes.

Your leads aren't moving through the sales process.

Even effective campaigns can lose value when new inquiries aren't captured, assigned, or followed up consistently.

I look beyond the ad account to understand how your campaigns, landing pages, tracking, and CRM work together. The goal is to improve acquisition efficiency and lead quality, not simply increase traffic or advertising spend.

BEFORE YOU START

Paid Advertising FAQs.

Practical answers about platforms, budgets, measurement, and campaign expectations.

Which advertising platforms do you manage?

I work with Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and relevant paid acquisition channels. The recommended platform depends on your offer, market, budget, and ideal customers.

Can you improve my existing advertising campaigns?

Yes. I can review campaign structure, targeting, keywords, creative, budgets, landing pages, conversion tracking, and lead quality to identify areas for improvement.

What advertising budget do I need?

The appropriate budget depends on your industry, location, competition, expected acquisition costs, and business objectives. I assess the economics and testing requirements before recommending a budget.

How do you measure paid advertising performance?

I review platform performance alongside completed leads, cost per lead, qualified opportunities, conversion rates, and sales outcomes when reliable tracking and CRM data are available.

Can you connect my advertising campaigns to GoHighLevel?

Yes. I can connect supported lead sources to GoHighLevel forms, pipelines, automated follow-ups, notifications, and reporting workflows when included in the project scope.

Can you guarantee a specific number of leads or ROAS?

No. Advertising performance depends on market conditions, audience demand, competition, budget, offer quality, landing pages, and sales execution. I focus on controlled campaign management, clear measurement, and ongoing optimization.
