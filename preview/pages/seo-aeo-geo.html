CLEAR ANSWERS

SEO, AEO & GEO FAQs.

Understand the approach, priorities, and practical limits before getting started.

What's the difference between SEO, AEO, and GEO?

SEO improves a website's accessibility, relevance, and visibility in traditional organic search. AEO focuses on making information clear and useful for direct answers. GEO focuses on helping generative search systems accurately understand and potentially reference a business through relevant content, consistent entities, and credible evidence.

These approaches share many of the same technical and content foundations.

Does my business need all three?

Not necessarily. The priority depends on your current website, target customers, competition, and business objectives. A strong technical and content foundation usually comes before specialized optimization.

How long does it take to see progress?

It depends on your existing website, market competition, technical issues, and the work required. Some changes can be evaluated quickly, while sustained growth in competitive search results may take months.

I focus on measurable progress rather than promising specific rankings or timelines.

Do you guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No. Search engines and AI platforms control their results. I can improve your website's technical foundation, content quality, structure, relevance, and supporting evidence, but specific rankings, AI mentions, and citations cannot be guaranteed.

How do you measure SEO's contribution to leads?

I review relevant organic search visibility alongside website conversions, completed inquiries, booked calls, and qualified leads where reliable tracking is available.

The goal is to understand how SEO contributes to meaningful business opportunities, not just increases in traffic.
